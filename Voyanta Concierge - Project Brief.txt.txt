# Project Brief – Bahamas Vacation Planning & Concierge Platform

## Project Vision

Create the premier online platform for planning vacations to The Bahamas.

The platform should allow travelers to plan every aspect of their trip in one place—from accommodations and airport transportation to excursions, restaurants, and local experiences.

Rather than functioning as a traditional travel agency, the platform should position itself as a *Bahamas Vacation Concierge*, providing personalized recommendations, itinerary planning, and access to trusted local businesses.

Our goal is to become the first website travelers think of when planning a trip to The Bahamas.

---

# Mission

Help travelers experience the very best of The Bahamas while making vacation planning simple, enjoyable, and stress-free.

Provide authentic local recommendations and trusted service providers, all in one platform.

# Cruise Passenger Experience

The platform should also provide a dedicated planning experience for cruise passengers visiting The Bahamas.

Cruise passengers have different needs from traditional vacation travelers because they may only have several hours available to explore the island before returning to their ship.

The platform should help cruise passengers find activities and experiences that reasonably fit within their available time in port.

Cruise passengers should be able to enter:

* Cruise line
* Cruise ship
* Arrival date
* Estimated docking time
* Ship departure or all-aboard time
* Number of travelers
* Adults
* Children
* Budget
* Interests

Examples of cruise passenger interests:

* Beaches
* Water activities
* Food
* Shopping
* Sightseeing
* History and culture
* Adventure
* Family activities
* Luxury experiences
* Relaxation

The platform should then recommend experiences based on the amount of time the passenger has available.

---

# Cruise Day Planner

The Cruise Day Planner should build a personalized itinerary specifically around the passenger's available port time.

Cruise itineraries should not fill every available hour.

Additional time should intentionally be left available for:

* Transportation
* Walking
* Meeting local providers
* Possible delays
* Returning to the cruise port
* Boarding the ship

For example, if a cruise passenger has approximately four hours available, the platform may recommend approximately two to three hours of planned activities instead of scheduling activities for the entire four-hour period.

Cruise experiences could be organized into categories such as:

* Under 3 Hours
* 3–4 Hours
* 4–6 Hours
* 6+ Hours

Passengers with shorter port visits should primarily be shown activities located closer to the cruise port.

Passengers with longer port visits can receive a wider selection of recommendations.

# Recommended Cruise Return Buffer

The platform should help cruise passengers plan conservatively.

Instead of scheduling activities until the ship's departure or all-aboard time, the platform should create a recommended activity window that ends earlier.

Example:

Ship docking time:

9:00 AM

Ship all-aboard time:

4:30 PM

Recommended experience window:

Approximately 10:00 AM–2:30 PM

This gives the traveler additional time to return toward the cruise port.

The exact recommended time may vary depending on:

* Activity
* Location
* Distance from the cruise port
* Transportation requirements
* Traffic
* Provider
* Other circumstances

The purpose of this feature is to help travelers make better scheduling decisions rather than guarantee that they will return to their cruise ship by a specific time.

# Independent Local Providers

During the initial stages of the platform, many cruise activities, tours, transportation services, excursions, and other experiences will be provided by independent local Bahamian businesses.

The platform should act as the planning, discovery, and concierge platform connecting travelers with trusted local businesses.

Voyanta Concierge should not represent itself as directly operating services that are actually being provided by independent businesses.

Cruise activity listings should clearly display information such as:

* Estimated duration
* Location
* Estimated distance or travel time from the cruise port
* Meeting location
* Transportation included or not included
* Recommended amount of available port time
* Provider information
* What's included
* What's not included
* Requirements
* Cancellation policy

---

# Cruise Passenger Return Disclaimer

The platform should NOT guarantee that cruise passengers will return to their ship by a specific time.

Because transportation, tours, excursions, and activities may be operated by independent local businesses, actual experience durations and transportation times may vary.

Cruise passengers remain responsible for knowing their cruise ship's official all-aboard and departure times and ensuring they return to the ship on time.

The platform's responsibility is to help travelers select activities that reasonably fit within their available time and encourage appropriate scheduling buffers.
